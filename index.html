Evaluate and pilot an RMM

  • Compare public RMM pricing to see which costs can be calculated from vendor disclosures and which require a quote.

  • Plan the first day of an RMM trial to enroll one authorized device, verify its check-in, and prove one safe workflow.
  • Measure alert-to-ticket timing during an RMM pilot using worked online and offline examples.
  • Prepare a support ticket for a missing first endpoint using the last setup step you verified.
  • Test diagnostics consent in Intune Remote Help before updating your helpdesk runbook.
  • Compare RMM tools with a two-client pilot to verify check-in, alert routing, and technician access.
  • Plan an RMM switch by site and use the Atera-to-Syncro worksheet to reconcile the old and new device records.
  • Verify offboarding on an offline laptop by tracking management removal and access revocation separately.
